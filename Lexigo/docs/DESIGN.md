# Lexigo Design System

Lexigo's visual language follows the playful, flat, highly tactile style made
popular by Duolingo: bright solid colours, chunky pressable buttons, a rounded
bold typeface and characters built from simple rounded shapes. **All assets are
original.** Lexigo has its own mascot (Lexi the chameleon), wordmark, font
(Nunito, OFL) and illustrations, and uses no Duolingo trademarks or artwork.

Tokens live in `scripts/ui/ui.gd`; illustrations are generated by
`tools/art/make_art.py`.

## Research notes

| Principle (from public sources) | How Lexigo applies it |
|---|---|
| Illustrations are built from three basic shapes: rounded rectangle (most common), circle and rounded triangle. Pointy shapes are off-brand ([design.duolingo.com](https://design.duolingo.com/illustration)) | `make_art.py` only has `rect()`, `circle()` and `tri()` (a triangle with rounded corners) |
| Use as few shapes as possible and vary their sizes for rhythm; a character's head and body are each 1–2 shapes ([characters](https://design.duolingo.com/illustration/characters)) | Lexi's head is one circle, the body one rounded rect, the tail two circles |
| Flat, friendly, minimal art, adopted partly so illustrations can be produced fast ([blog](https://blog.duolingo.com/shape-language-duolingos-art-style/)) | No outlines or gradients; one highlight and one shade shape per form |
| One saturated green carries the primary call to action; blue for selection/info, red for errors, yellow for rewards ([open-design](https://github.com/nexu-io/open-design/blob/main/design-systems/duolingo/DESIGN.md), [shadcn.io](https://www.shadcn.io/design/duolingo)) | `LEAF` is used only for "continue/check/success" |
| Tactile buttons: a 4dp darker bottom lip that collapses when pressed; 12–16dp radii ([refero](https://styles.refero.design/style/7088d695-362b-4e09-b325-fa8136d4f350)) | `UI.button()` with an 8px lip (the 720px viewport is ≈2× dp) |
| Rounded heavy display type, uppercase tracked button labels | Nunito 800 with glyph spacing for caps |
| Mascots carry the emotion; prompts are spoken by a character | Lexi asks for each clue from a speech bubble and reacts to right and wrong answers |

## Components

* **Button.** Radius 26; `LIP` 8px darker bottom border. Pressed: the lip
  disappears, the face drops 8px and the label moves with it. Kinds:
  `primary` (leaf), `secondary` (sky), `danger`, `reward`, `special`, `outline`,
  `neutral` (white, 4px `CLOUD` border).
* **Card.** White, radius 32, 4px `CLOUD` border plus a 2px extra bottom lip.
  When selected it turns `SKY_PALE` with a `#84D8FF` border.
* **Crossword tile.** White with a `CLOUD` border and lip. Active word
  `#F1FAFF`/`#84D8FF`, cursor `SKY_PALE`/`SKY`, solved `LEAF_PALE`/`#A5ED6E` with
  `LEAF_LIP` letters, wrong flash `BERRY_PALE`/`BERRY`, celebration shimmer `SUN`.
* **Keyboard key.** A neutral button, radius 18. The accent row is `SKY_PALE`.
* **Progress bar.** `CLOUD` track, `LEAF` fill, with a white 30% highlight stripe.
* **Level node.** A 116px circle with a 12px lip, in the world colour (`CLOUD`
  when locked). It shows a star when done, a crown on the boss level, and a
  bobbing `START` bubble plus a soft ring on the current level.
* **Feedback strip.** Slides up over the keyboard: `LEAF_PALE` with "Nice!" or
  `BERRY_PALE` with "Not quite!", then slides away after 1.1 s.
* **Stat cards (results).** A coloured header with a caps title above a white
  inner panel: XP (`SUN`), time (`SKY`), accuracy (`LEAF`).
* **Switch.** A 104×60 pill track (`SKY` on, `CLOUD` off) with a white knob that has a lip.

## Illustration rules

1. Only rounded rectangles, circles and rounded triangles; strokes only for
   thin details (smiles, motion lines), always with round caps.
2. Flat fills from the palette: one lighter highlight (white at 30–35%)
   top-left, and one darker shade at the bottom of each form.
3. No outlines, no gradients, no textures.
4. Badges: 144px rounded squares (radius 40) with a 12% black drop lip and a highlight pill.
5. Icons: 64px canvas, chunky and readable at 32px.

### Mascot: Lexi the chameleon

A chameleon changes colour the way a learner switches language. Poses:
`happy` (holding an "A" tile), `wave`, `cheer`, `think` (with a ? bubble) and
`oops` (with a sweat drop). The game shows `think` while you work on a clue,
`cheer` when you solve a word or finish a grid, and `oops` when an answer is wrong.

## Motion

* Tiles bounce when typed (0.32 s sine), shimmer gold when their word is solved,
  and flash red when it's wrong.
* Buttons press down by their lip; level nodes and the `START` bubble bob gently.
* Results: the card slides up with an overshoot, stars land one by one with a
  rising pitch, and counters roll up over 0.8 s.

```sh
python3 tools/art/make_art.py                                         # SVGs

```
