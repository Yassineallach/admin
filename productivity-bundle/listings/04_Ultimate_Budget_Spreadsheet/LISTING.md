## FAQ (add to the shop's FAQ section or answer in messages)

**Can I start in the middle of the year?** Yes. Choose the first month of your budget on Easy Setup; the 12 month tabs follow from there.

**Does it connect to my bank?** No. It's a private spreadsheet: you type or paste your transactions, and nothing leaves your computer.

**Which currencies work?** Any. Pick from the list on Easy Setup, or just type amounts in your own currency.

**Do I need Microsoft Excel?** No. The Google Sheets edition works with a free Google account.

**Does it contain macros?** No. Standard .xlsx files with formulas only.

**Can I get a refund?** Digital downloads usually can't be returned. Message me if anything doesn't work and I'll help.
