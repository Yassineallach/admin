## FAQ (add to the shop's FAQ section or answer in messages)

**Does it work in OneNote for Mac or iPad?** Microsoft only lets OneNote for Windows open .onepkg files. On Mac, web and iPad, insert the PDF as printouts. If you set `onenote_shared_notebook_link` in `listing_config.json`, START_HERE.pdf also gives buyers a link to copy the notebook to their own OneDrive.

**Do the links work in GoodNotes / Notability?** Yes. The tabs, index and contents links are PDF hyperlinks. Use read mode, or tap with your finger rather than the pen.

**Is it 2027 only?** The calendars and weekly pages are dated for 2027. The templates and notes are undated and reusable.

**Can I duplicate pages?** Yes, in OneNote (right-click the page › Copy, then Paste) and in every PDF note app.

**Can I get a refund?** Digital downloads usually can't be returned. Message me if anything doesn't work and I'll help.
