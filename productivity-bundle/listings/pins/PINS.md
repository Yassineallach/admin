# Pinterest pins

Three pins per listing, 1000 x 1500 px: a **product** pin (the name as the headline), a **question** pin (what buyers type into Pinterest search, answered by the sample file) and an **inside** pin (three real screens). Three different images send three times the fresh-pin signal of one image pinned three times.

How to post: upload the image, paste the title, description and alt text, choose the board, and set the link to that product's Etsy listing. `pins_schedule.csv` has the same copy in Pinterest's bulk-upload format (add your Etsy links and the image URLs, or post by hand from it). Two pins a day keeps the account active without flooding followers.

## Boards

Create these boards first and give each a one-line description that uses its name:

- **Budget Spreadsheets & Money Templates**
- **Debt Payoff & Saving Money**
- **Planner & Productivity Templates**
- **Small Business Pricing & Profit Templates**
- **Excel & Google Sheets Templates**: save every pin here as well, as a second board.
