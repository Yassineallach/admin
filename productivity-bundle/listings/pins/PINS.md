# Pinterest pins

One pin per listing, 1000 × 1500 px. For each pin: upload the image, paste the title and description, and set the link to that product's Etsy listing URL.

Suggested boards: *Budget Spreadsheets & Money Trackers*, *Planner & Productivity Templates*, *Excel & Google Sheets Templates*. Pin one or two a day instead of all at once, and re-pin the best performers to a second board.
