# Brass Ledger — Launch Schedule

A four-week plan to open the shop and publish all 11 listings. Dates assume you start on Wednesday 11 November 2026; shift every date by the same number of days if you start later. Each listing's text, photos and buyer files are in its own folder.

## Before you start (one evening)

1. Open one Excel file and one Google Sheets file from each product on your own computer. Check that it opens without a repair message, dates read correctly and the sidebar links work.
2. Open the planner PDF in the note app you use and tap a few links.
3. Upload the Google Sheets files to Google Drive, save each as a Google Sheet and build the "make a copy" links (steps are in each LISTING.md checklist).

## Week 4 — review and adjust

| Day | Date | Do this |
|---|---|---|
| Tue | 1 Dec | Look at Etsy Stats: views, visits and favourites per listing. |
| Wed | 2 Dec | For listings with views but no visits, try a different first photo or reorder the title's first words. |
| Thu | 3 Dec | Consider Etsy Ads at a small daily budget on the two listings with the most favourites. |
| Fri | 4 Dec | Re-pin your two best pins to a second board. |
| Mon | 7 Dec | Plan the January push: budgets, habit trackers and planners sell best in the first weeks of the year. |

## Every week after that

- Pin three to five times a week.
- Answer messages within a day.
- After each sale, send a short thank-you and ask for a review once the buyer has had a few days with the file.
- Raise prices from launch to list level once a listing has about five reviews.

## Order to publish, and why

1. Planner 2027: seasonal, so it goes first.
2. Budget Spreadsheet and Weekly Planner Pro: the widest demand.
3. Project Manager, then the bundle: the bundle needs the three singles live.
4. Small trackers: low-priced entry products that point to the Budget Spreadsheet.

One listing a day keeps the shop looking active and gives each listing its own "new" window in search.
