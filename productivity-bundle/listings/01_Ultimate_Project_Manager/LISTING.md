## FAQ (add to the shop's FAQ section or answer in messages)

**Do I need Microsoft Excel?** No. The Google Sheets edition works with a free Google account. Excel desktop gives the full rounded-card design.

**Will it work on my iPad or phone?** You can view it in the Excel or Google Sheets apps, but set-up and data entry are much easier on a computer.

**Can I use it for more than one project?** Yes. It holds 100 projects and 500 tasks. For a separate workspace, make a copy of the file.

**Can I change the categories, statuses or team members?** Yes, on the Setup tab. Every dropdown reads from there.

**Does it contain macros?** No. The files are standard .xlsx with formulas only.

**Can I get a refund?** Because the files are delivered instantly, digital downloads usually can't be returned. Message me if anything doesn't work and I'll help.
