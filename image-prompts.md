# Red Rising — image prompts (Axiom Lab)

One art style for every image, so the video looks like one production.

## 1. The style: "Imperial Mars" painterly concept art

Dark, cinematic, painted concept art: visible oil-paint brushwork, strong light and shadow, muted desaturated backgrounds, with **crimson** and **gold** as the only strong colours. Think of a big-budget film's concept paintings, not a photo and not anime. The Roman-empire touches (marble, laurels, eagles, togas mixed with armour) fit the Gold culture of the books.

**Style suffix**: paste this at the end of every prompt:

```
painterly cinematic concept art, visible oil-paint brushwork, dramatic chiaroscuro lighting, muted desaturated palette with crimson and gold accents, dark sci-fi with Roman imperial influence, atmospheric haze, subtle film grain, highly detailed, 16:9 widescreen composition
```

**Negative prompt**: for tools that support one:

```
text, letters, captions, watermark, signature, logo, book cover, frame, border, cartoon, anime, 3D render, plastic skin, oversaturated, gore, blood spray, extra fingers, deformed hands
```

**Settings**
- Aspect ratio **16:9**. In Midjourney add `--ar 16:9 --style raw`; in other tools set 1920×1080 or larger.
- **Portraits:** put the subject on the **right third**. The left and lower-left are kept clear for the name titles.
- Keep a **seed or style reference** from your first good image and reuse it. That keeps everything consistent.
- **★ = layered** (A01, A02, A03, A08, A15, A20, A23, A30, A31, A32): if your tool can, generate a version without the foreground character, or separate foreground from background, for the parallax camera moves.
- **No real actors' names or likenesses.** The characters below are original descriptions.
- **A07 and A33** are the official book covers. Use the real covers; don't generate them.
