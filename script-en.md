# Red Rising — narration script (English, “dear friend” style)

Channel: Axiom Lab · first person · shot numbers match the storyboard.


## Cold open · 0:00

**S01** Dear friend, before we get into any details, I want you to picture something. You’re sixteen years old, you live underground on Mars, and your whole job is drilling into rock.

**S02** Meet Darrow. He’s a Helldiver — basically the most dangerous gig in the mines — and he is really, really good at it.

**S03** And every scrap of helium-3 he digs up? He’s told it’s helping turn Mars into a home for future generations. Pretty noble, right?

**S04** Wrong, my friend. It’s a lie. Mars has been livable for ages. Up on the surface? Cities. Gardens. A whole empire.

**S05** And the people running it built every inch of it on the backs of people like Darrow.

**S06** Welcome to Red Rising.


## Title · 0:46

**S07** (no voice-over)

**S08** Oh, right — I forgot to introduce myself. Welcome to Axiom Lab. Today I’m breaking down Pierce Brown’s Red Rising trilogy: how this world works, my honest take with no spoilers, and then, for the brave ones only, the whole story. And stick around to the end — I’ve got a question for you.


## 01 The Society · 1:04

**S09** So, first things first: the world. Centuries from now, humanity has spread across the solar system, and it’s run by something called the Society. Its big rule? Every single person is born into a Color.

**S10** And your Color decides everything. Imagine your birth certificate already listing your job, your marriage and your future — before you’ve even opened your eyes.

**S11** Silvers handle the money. Coppers push the paperwork. Blues fly the ships, Greens run the tech, Grays keep the peace, Obsidians fight the wars, and Pinks… well, they’re there to serve those above them.

**S12** Sitting right at the top? The Golds. Bigger, faster, smarter — literally engineered to rule. And the best of the best even carry a scar to prove it. They’re called the Peerless Scarred.

**S13** And way down at the bottom are the Reds. They dig, they don’t ask questions, and they’re expected to be grateful for the scraps.

**S14** Golds rule because they’re the best. Reds dig because that’s what they’re for. That’s the Society’s whole pitch: it’s not a choice — it’s just nature.

**S15** And Red Rising, my friend, is the story of the moment one Red stops believing it… and decides to burn the whole system down.


## 02 The review · 2:19

**S16** Okay — so is it actually worth reading? Let’s get into it.

**S17** Book one, Red Rising, is the most familiar of the bunch. Outsider kid, brutal school, teenagers at war. And yes — you’re going to think of The Hunger Games and Ender’s Game. What’s that? You thought of them in the first minute? Don’t worry, everybody does. But this one is meaner, bloodier, and way more interested in who holds the power.

**S18** What really sells it, though, is the pace. Short chapters, cliffhanger after cliffhanger — and just when you think you know what kind of story this is, it changes on you.

**S19** Then there’s Golden Son, and this is where things go to a whole other level. Space fleets, ruling houses, backstabbing galore. A lot of fans call it the best of the three — and that ending? People are still talking about it.

**S20** And Morning Star brings it all home: all-out war, massive battles, and the bill for everything that came before finally comes due.

**S21** But honestly? What’ll keep you hooked is the cast. Sevro, Mustang, Cassius, Roque, Victra, Ragnar — they start out as types and turn into people you will absolutely argue about.

**S22** Now hold on, it’s not perfect. Darrow is good at… pretty much everything, to the point where you’ll want to tell him: okay man, take a break. The made-up slang — bloodydamn, gorydamn — takes some getting used to. And fair warning: it is violent. Very violent.

**S23** But under all the swords and starships, there’s real substance here: how power justifies itself, what it costs to fight back, and whether you can pretend to be your enemy without turning into them.

**S24** So, my verdict? Red Rising gets an eight out of ten. Golden Son, a nine and a half. Morning Star, a solid nine. Together? One of the most addictive sci-fi series of the last decade — and I’m speaking from experience.

**S25** If you love fast, high-stakes, political sci-fi with characters you’ll get way too attached to — pick it up. If you’re after slow, hard-science worldbuilding… this one might not be for you.

## 03 Red Rising · 4:44

**S27** Still here? Good, I knew you wouldn’t leave. It all kicks off with Darrow’s wife, Eo. She believes their people deserve way more than a life of taking orders.

**S28** So she sings a forbidden song — in public. The Society hangs her for it. Darrow buries her, which is also a crime… so they hang him too.

**S29** Except — he wakes up. The Sons of Ares, a Red resistance group, saved him. And they’ve got a plan nobody has ever pulled off.

**S30** Enter Mickey, a Carver, who rebuilds Darrow into a Gold. New bones, new body, golden eyes, new manners — even a new name: Darrow au Andromedus.

**S31** The mission? Get into the Institute, climb the Gold ranks, and tear the whole system down from the inside.

**S32** Step one is the Passage, and it is brutal: every student has to kill another to make it through. Darrow kills Julian au Bellona — who just happens to be the brother of his new friend, Cassius. Yeah. That one comes back to haunt him.

**S33** From there, Darrow lands in House Mars, and the students get thrown into a war game across a massive valley. The rules? Basically nonexistent. And the referees — the Proctors? Fair? Come on, my friend.

**S34** So Darrow builds an army out of misfits. There’s Sevro — wild, weird, and loyal to the end…

**S35** …and Mustang, a sharp-as-a-tack girl from House Minerva who becomes his ally — and, well, a lot more than that.

**S36** And how does he win? By doing the one thing nobody saw coming: he takes the fight to the Proctors themselves, storming Olympus and proving the whole game was rigged.

**S37** His last obstacle is the Jackal — Adrius au Augustus, Mustang’s twin brother and son of the ArchGovernor of Mars. Darrow beats him… and takes his hand.

**S38** And his reward? He becomes the protégé of Nero au Augustus — the most powerful man on Mars, and the very man who ordered Eo’s execution. Just imagine how that feels.


## 04 Golden Son · 6:52

**S39** Golden Son kicks off with Darrow losing — big. He drops a fleet battle at the Academy, and with it, Nero’s support.

**S40** So what does he do? He beats the Golds at their own game, pitting House Augustus against House Bellona until it all boils over into war.

**S41** He rounds up allies — Victra au Julii, Roque, Sevro and his Howlers — and racks up win after win.

**S42** Then comes his big victory party, the Triumph. Which is exactly when everything collapses.

**S43** Roque — his closest friend among the Golds — turns on him.

**S44** The Jackal has teamed up with the Sovereign, Octavia au Lune. And he knows exactly what Darrow really is.

**S45** And the biggest gut-punch? Fitchner — Sevro’s dad, and Darrow’s old Proctor — was Ares, leader of the Sons, all along. And the Jackal kills him.

**S46** By the last page, Darrow’s been captured, his friends are scattered or dead, and the revolution is in pieces. Talk about a cliffhanger.


## 05 Morning Star · 8:01

**S47** Morning Star picks up with Darrow in the dark — literally. He’s spent months as the Jackal’s prisoner.

**S48** That is, until Sevro shows up to bust him out — now wearing his father’s helm as the new Ares.

**S49** And now the rebellion goes public. Darrow and Sevro free the Obsidians, led by Ragnar Volarus, turning the Society’s scariest soldiers against it.

**S50** Mustang throws her own forces into the fight.

**S51** And even Cassius — the guy whose brother Darrow killed — ends up on his side.

**S52** But none of it comes cheap. Ragnar falls. And Roque, beaten, takes his own life.

**S53** It all comes down to Luna, the Sovereign’s seat of power — and that’s where the Jackal’s war finally ends.

**S54** The Society falls. A brand-new Solar Republic rises, with Mustang leading it. And Darrow finally gets to meet his son, Pax.


## 06 Final thoughts · 9:04

**S55** So, the bottom line, my friend? Red Rising starts out as a revenge story, but it ends with a much harder question: okay, the revolution won… now what?

**S56** Is it perfect? Nope. But very few series move this fast while asking questions this big.

**S57** Remember that question I promised you? Here it is. Tell me in the comments: who’s the real hero of this trilogy — Darrow, Sevro, or Mustang? And I want real reasons, not ‘because he looks cool’.

**S58** And if you’ve already finished it, good news: the story keeps going. Iron Gold, Dark Age and Light Bringer pick up ten years later.
